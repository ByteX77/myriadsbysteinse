BEHIND THE LENS

About me.

I'm Steinse, a wildlife and nature photographer from Kerala, India — God's Own Country.

I love travelling, exploring new places, and discovering the incredible beauty of wildlife. From colourful birds to the hidden wonders of forests, I'm always looking for moments worth capturing through my lens.

Photography is more than just a hobby to me. It's a way to tell stories, connect with nature, and share the beauty of the world with others.

I'm also passionate about wildlife conservation and protecting the natural habitats that countless species call home. Through Myriads by Steinse, I hope to celebrate nature, share my adventures, and inspire others to appreciate and protect our planet.

Explore. Capture. Conserve. Stay wild.
